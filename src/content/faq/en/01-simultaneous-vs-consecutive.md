---
lang: en
question: What is the difference between simultaneous and consecutive interpreting?
order: 1
page: booth-or-portable
---

In **simultaneous**, the interpreter renders while the speaker is still
talking, a few seconds behind. The event runs to the length it was scheduled
for. It requires equipment: a booth or a portable system.

In **consecutive**, the speaker talks for a stretch and pauses for the
interpreter. It needs no equipment at all, but it **doubles the running time**.
A two-hour meeting becomes four.

The choice almost always resolves into two questions: can the agenda run to
twice its length, and how many people need to hear it. If the answer is “it
cannot double” or “more than two people”, it is simultaneous.

One vocabulary note: in Brazil almost everyone says *tradução simultânea*
(literally “simultaneous translation”). The technical term is *interpretação* —
translation is written, interpreting is spoken. Nobody will correct you, but if
you meet both terms, they mean the same thing here.
