One for every person who **does not understand the speaker's language**. Not
the total number of people at the event.

This is the most common counting error. At an 80-person event where only 12
attendees are foreign, you need 12 receivers, not 80.

Two practical recommendations:

- **Ask for two or three more** than the exact count. Someone always decides on
  the day that they would rather follow through the earpiece.
- **Count per language separately.** If the event has English and Spanish, those
  are two distinct listener groups and possibly two channels.

We own 30 receivers. Up to that number the equipment is ours and there is no
hire line in the quote. Above it, we hire the difference and it appears
itemised.
