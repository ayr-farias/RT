---
lang: en
question: Does the room need special audio?
order: 5
page: booth-or-portable
---

It depends on the format, and the answer varies a lot.

**Portable system:** nothing required. The interpreter carries their own
transmitter. If the room already has a mic and speakers for the presenter, good,
but it is not a prerequisite.

**Booth:** yes, and it is the item that most surprises first-time buyers. The
booth needs to receive audio from the room's sound desk — it is not enough for
the interpreter to hear the room through the air. That means the event needs a
PA with a dedicated output for interpretation, and a technician who knows how to
deliver it.

**Remote event:** the requirement moves out of the room and onto each speaker's
microphone. It is the most critical of the three, and the cheapest to fix: a
basic headset on whoever is speaking solves most of it.

In all three cases, a short test beforehand pays for itself. Twenty minutes of
technical rehearsal prevents most of what otherwise surfaces mid-event.
