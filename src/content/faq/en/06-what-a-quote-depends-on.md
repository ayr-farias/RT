question: What does a quote depend on?
order: 6
page: booth-or-portable
---

Five things, in order of weight:

1. **The date.** Availability decides everything. A date confirmed well ahead
   usually prices better than a last-minute one, and in conference season
   calendars close early.
2. **The duration.** Half day, full day, or several days. The interpreter day
   rate is the market's standard unit.
3. **The room format.** This is what decides whether the job uses portable
   equipment (ours, no hire) or a booth (hire, technician, rigging). It is the
   largest price difference between two events of the same length.
4. **How many people will listen to interpretation.** Up to 30, the equipment is
   ours. Above that, receiver hire enters the quote.
5. **How many language pairs.** Each additional pair is an additional pair of
   interpreters.

Beyond that: travel and accommodation, when the event is outside Greater Recife.

We do not publish a price list because the combination of those five variables
moves the number easily. The quote form asks exactly these points, and with them
answered the first reply comes back with a number.
