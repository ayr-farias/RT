## The three modes

**Consecutive.** The speaker talks for a stretch, pauses, and the interpreter
renders it. It doubles the length of the meeting, but needs no equipment at all
and works anywhere — including standing up in the middle of a building site.

**Whispered (*chuchotage*).** The interpreter sits beside one or two people and
renders in a very low voice, in real time. It works for up to two people. Above
that it does not: the voice would have to rise enough to disturb the table.

**Escort.** The interpreter accompanies the person or delegation through the
day — visit, lunch, transfer, meeting — switching between consecutive and
whispered as the situation requires.

## When to choose this over simultaneous

- Meetings of up to two hours with few participants
- Interviews, depositions, legal meetings
- Site visits with two or three visitors
- Dinners, receptions, transfers
- Any situation where doubling the running time is not a problem

If the event has a tight agenda, a lot of people listening, or cannot run to
twice the length, the answer is simultaneous — portable or with a booth,
depending on the room.

## What is included

The interpreter, and nothing else. No equipment, no rigging, no logistics. It
is the simplest mode to book and the least expensive, when the format allows it.
